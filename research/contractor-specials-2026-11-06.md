# Arizona Contractor Specials Sweep — Yuma & Phoenix Metro

*Generated 2026-11-06. All specials are time-sensitive and must be re-verified before use (call the company and confirm terms in writing with an expiration date).*

## About this report

A web sweep (WebSearch) for currently advertised specials from licensed home-improvement
contractors in the **Yuma** and **Phoenix** metro areas, across interior/exterior remodel,
flooring, painting, cabinetry, countertops, tile/stone, roofing, doors/windows, and
landscaping/xeriscape.

**Verification notes — read before using:**
- Every entry below has a real source URL. Nothing is listed without one.
- **ROC license numbers are as stated on each company's own website/listing. Direct
  verification at azroc.gov was NOT possible in this session** (the contractor-search tool
  at azroc.gov returns an interactive lookup form, not per-license status, to an automated
  fetch). Each ROC number is therefore marked **unverified** and must be checked at
  <https://azroc.gov> → Contractor Search before relying on it. Where a company printed a
  self-reported expiration (e.g. "EXP 02/28/2027"), that is noted but is still self-reported.
- Ratings/review counts are from third-party aggregators or company pages and drift over
  time; several sources conflict. Treat them as indicative, not exact.
- Entries marked **(stale)** had cached pages hundreds of days old — likely expired; kept
  only because the offer text was explicit. Verify before quoting to a client.
- Total current/dated entries: **22** (16 competitors + 6 partner/material-store leads).
